Verdex is a student-run sampling effort. We record microplastic particle counts across Long Island’s coastal waters under a documented, version-locked protocol.

Latest finding · Session 4 · October 11, 2026

Every station we sampled at Wantagh Park had suspected microplastics.

In our first session under VSS v1.2, we sieved 15 liters of water at each of five stations. All five returned suspected microplastic particles: 153 in total, and 145 of them were fibers, the kind that shed from synthetic clothing in the wash.

Each mark is one particle we counted at that station, drawn in the color we recorded under the microscope.

What this means

Particles consistent with microplastics are present in the water at Wantagh Park, at every point we sampled. Fibers have also been the most common particle type in every earlier full session.

What it doesn’t mean yet

These are visual identifications checked with a hot needle, not polymer confirmations; confirming the plastic type needs spectroscopy. This is one session at one site, and counts are not directly comparable to the 1-liter v1.1 sessions.

Method · VSS Protocol v1.2

One protocol, version-locked.

The Verdex Sampling Standard (VSS) is informed by NOAA marine-debris monitoring guidance and by outside methodology feedback. Each version is locked before the sessions that use it, so sessions under the same version stay comparable. v1.1 covered Sessions 1–3. v1.2, which moved from 1-liter jars to 15-liter sieved samples after reviewers flagged sample volume, applies from Session 4.

Step 01

Collect

At each of five stations, water is collected about 10 cm below the surface and poured through a 100 µm stainless-steel sieve until 15 liters have passed through. Nitrile gloves throughout. Site, time, tide stage, and weather are recorded on the field sheet at the moment of collection.

Step 02

Blank

Every session carries a blank suite: field blank, reagent blank, airborne control, lab sieve carry-over, and a field sieve rinse between stations, plus a rotating field duplicate or transport blank. If a blank is dirty, the session's counts are reported with that blank value attached — never quietly dropped.

Step 03

Recover

Material caught on the sieve is rinsed with distilled water into a covered glass jar, then filtered and dried in a covered workspace with foil-lined trays before examination.

Step 04

Count

Particles are counted and typed as fiber, fragment, film, foam, or pellet under an Accu-Scope EXS-210-13 stereo microscope, with a hot-needle check on ambiguous particles. Counts and photos are entered into the Verdex sheet the same day.

Single-team disclosure

All samples to date were collected, processed, and counted by one team using the VSS protocol (v1.1 for Sessions 1–3, v1.2 from Session 4). No independent laboratory has re-counted a Verdex sample, and inter-observer agreement has not yet been quantified. Counts are visual identifications, not polymer confirmations.

Verdex will not recruit additional teams until it has completed six consecutive protocol-compliant sessions and closed at least one external methodology review.
